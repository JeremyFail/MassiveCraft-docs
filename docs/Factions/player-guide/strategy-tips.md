# Strategy & Tips

Master the art of faction warfare, from raiding enemy bases to defending your territory. This guide covers combat strategies, power management, and tips for becoming a dominant faction on your server.

## Warfare and Strategy

### Raiding

To raid an enemy faction:

1. **Reduce their power** by killing their members
2. **Claim their territory** when vulnerable
3. **Defend your claims** from counter-attacks

### Defending

To protect your faction:

1. **Keep your power high** - avoid unnecessary deaths
2. **Have more members** to maintain higher total power
3. **Build secure bases** in claimed territory
4. **Maintain alliances** for mutual protection

### Power Management

- **Stay online** to regenerate power over time
- **Avoid death** as it significantly reduces power
- **Recruit members** to increase total faction power
- **Claim strategically** - don't overextend beyond your power; consider not claiming all possible land to provide a safety buffer.

## Tips and Best Practices

### For New Players

1. **Join an established faction first** to learn the ropes
2. **Ask questions** - most faction leaders are happy to help
3. **Contribute to your faction** - help gather resources, build, and defend
4. **Learn the commands** - start with the basics and expand from there
5. **Understand power** - it's the most important resource

### For Faction Leaders

1. **Set clear rules** for your faction members
2. **Use the MOTD** to communicate important information
3. **Manage permissions** carefully to prevent griefing
4. **Maintain good relationships** with potential allies
5. **Plan your claims** strategically - quality over quantity
6. **Set up a faction home** in a secure location
7. **Create warps** for important locations
8. **Monitor your power** regularly with `/f status`

### Building in Claimed Territory

1. **Always claim before building** - unclaimed builds can be destroyed
2. **Build away from chunk borders** to avoid edge cases
3. **Use `/f seechunk`** to visualize chunk boundaries
4. **Create a buffer zone** of claimed chunks around your base
5. **Don't build too close to enemies** unless prepared for war

### Diplomacy

1. **Communicate with other factions** before taking hostile actions
2. **Honor your alliances** - reputation matters
3. **Use truces** when you want peace but not full alliance
4. **Document agreements** in faction descriptions or MOTD
5. **Be strategic** about enemy declarations
